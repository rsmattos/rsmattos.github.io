## Research & Software

Collection of projects I touched during my academic carrer, covers from programs where I was the main developer to projects made small contributions.

- **Newton-X**: Software package for molecular spectrum generation and [FSSH dynamics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surface_hopping).  
  [Website](https://newtonx.org/) · [Repo](https://gitlab.com/light-and-molecules/newton-x-package) · [Docs](https://light-and-molecules.gitlab.io/newton-x-package/) · [Paper](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/acs.jctc.2c00804)

- **Legion** — Gaussian wavepacket dynamics platform.  
  [Repo](https://gitlab.com/light-and-molecules/legion) · [Docs](https://gitlab.com/light-and-molecules/legion/-/blob/main/doc/manual/manual.pdf?ref_type=heads) · [Paper](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/acs.jctc.4c01697)

- **QDCT** - Post process treatment of FSSH trajectories.  
  [Repo](https://gitlab.com/rafaelcpii93/qdct) · [Paper](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/acs.jctc.4c00783)

- **ULaMDyn** - Analysis/visualization tools for [AIMD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mixed_quantum-classical_dynamics).  
  [Repo](https://gitlab.com/light-and-molecules/ulamdyn) · [Docs](https://ulamdyn.com/) · [Paper](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlelanding/2025/dd/d4dd00374h)
